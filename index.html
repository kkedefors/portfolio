I'm a Swedish and Korean-American freelance journalist based in the San Francisco Bay Area, reporting on international news, culture and power.

Since 2025 I've reported from China, South Korea, Japan, Thailand and the U.S., filing written stories alongside my own photographs and edited video. For most of the last two years, I've been on the road.

Before that I was a correspondent for The Boston Globe, covering arts, nightlife and breaking metro news, and an arts and breaking news reporter at The Dallas Morning News. At GBH News, my reporting on the Freedom Trail's ties to slavery contributed to the Freedom Trail Foundation updating its tour guide training. I've also worked as a podcast intern at E2AC and a research intern at NextEd.

My work has also appeared in Design Observer, D Magazine, KQED and 48 Hills. At Boston University I earned a joint major in philosophy and psychology and a second major in independent studies, a social science and public policy curriculum I designed, with a minor in journalism.

I grew up in the Bay Area, tended bar in Sweden and spent six months in Barcelona and Madrid studying Spanish linguistics and culture. Off the clock: stand-up comedy, memoirs, running, Afrobeats and wine. My goal is to listen to every episode of This American Life. I'm still working on it.
